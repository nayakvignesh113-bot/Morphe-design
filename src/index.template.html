What the numbers mean.
  • 2D plane-stress, linear-elastic, static analysis of a plate of uniform thickness. Stress is element-centroid von Mises; elements touching a point load are excluded from the peak because a point load has no finite stress. Your fastener detail sets that local stress.
  • Thickness is solved exactly: stress scales with 1/t and deflection with 1/(E·t), then rounded up to the next standard plate gauge.
  • Not checked: buckling of slender compression members, out-of-plane loads, fatigue, contact at bolt holes. Before release, rerun the exported geometry in your FEA package.
  • Solver verified against the published 88-line topology code (identical compliance history) and Timoshenko beam theory (tip deflection within 0.1%).
